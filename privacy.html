PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026 • Client-Side Architecture

At CraftCV, privacy is not an afterthought or a compliance checkbox—it is the foundational architectural pillar of our platform. We believe that your career trajectory, personal identifiers, contact coordinates, and professional accomplishments belong exclusively to you.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

CraftCV operates entirely within your browser client sandbox. All resume compilation, dynamic template rendering, typography sizing calculations, and ATS keyword scoring algorithms execute on your device's local CPU. We do not transmit your resume details to remote servers, cloud databases, or third-party AI endpoints.

2. Local Storage Persistence

When you edit your resume or switch between templates, your inputs are saved to your browser's native localStorage database. This ensures you do not lose your work if you accidentally refresh the tab or close your browser. This data resides solely on your physical device, and you have complete authority to erase it at any moment by clearing your browser data or clicking the Reset button in the wizard.

3. Zero Telemetry & Zero Tracking

CraftCV does not use tracking cookies, cross-site trackers, behavioral heatmaps, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party advertising services. We respect Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals by maintaining a clean zero-tracking runtime.

4. PDF Generation & Print Isolation

When you export a PDF, CraftCV utilizes your browser's native print engine. No documents or rasterized assets are ever routed through external PDF rendering microservices or cloud printers.

5. Inquiries & Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy architecture or open-source codebase, please contact us at help@ceimran.in.
